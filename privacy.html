English

Kampong Kitani is made for young children. We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no names, no birthdays.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party software development kits.
  • The app does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.
  • Progress (stickers, pictures, settings) is saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.
  • The parents’ area is protected by a grown-up check. Any link that leaves the app is inside the parents’ area.

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.

  • Tiada akaun, tiada log masuk, tiada nama dan tiada tarikh lahir.

  • Pembelian diuruskan sepenuhnya oleh Apple melalui App Store. Kami tidak melihat butiran pembayaran anda.
  • Ruang ibu bapa dilindungi oleh semakan orang dewasa. Semua pautan keluar dari aplikasi berada di ruang ibu bapa.

Jika dasar ini berubah, kami akan mengemas kini halaman ini dan tarikh di atas.
